# Citing pyradix

If you use pyradix in a publication, please cite the software. The **Cite this repository** button on
[GitHub](https://github.com/ar1so/pyradix) (generated from `CITATION.cff`) gives BibTeX and APA, including the exact
version you used: report it with `pyradix --version` or `pyradix.__version__`.

Please also cite the standard you followed:

- **`mode="ibsi"`**: Zwanenburg A, Vallières M, et al. *The Image Biomarker Standardization Initiative:
  standardized quantitative radiomics for high-throughput image-based phenotyping.* Radiology. 2020;295(2):328–338.
- **`mode="pyradiomics"`**: van Griethuysen JJM, Fedorov A, et al. *Computational Radiomics System to Decode the
  Radiographic Phenotype.* Cancer Research. 2017;77(21):e104–e107.

For the methods section, `dump_clear_yaml(result, path)` writes the extraction settings in the structure of the CLEAR
checklist (Kocak B, et al. *CheckList for EvaluAtion of Radiomics research (CLEAR).* Insights into Imaging.
2023;14:75).
